# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive
- Welcome newcomers and help them learn
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
